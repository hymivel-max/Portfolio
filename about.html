Chapter I

Roots in Chavara South, Kollam

Growing up in Thekkumbhagam, Chavara South (Kollam, Kerala 691584), surrounded by the tranquil waters of Ashtamudi and coastal Kerala landscapes, I discovered computing not merely as a hobby, but as a digital canvas. While most high school students were focused solely on textbooks, I was fascinated by how computer graphics work under the hood.

I began by modifying Minecraft shaders, experimenting with real-time lighting math, writing GLSL fragment shaders, and hosting custom game servers. That laid the bedrock for high-performance systems thinking.

Chapter II

Full-Stack Cloud & Mobile Architecture

Next, I transitioned into full-stack engineering. I built Pacha.leaf, a comprehensive botanical commerce ecosystem with Express 5, JWT authentication, and automated GitHub Actions CI/CD pipelines.

Using Capacitor 8, I packaged cross-platform mobile apps for Android and iOS, handling native permission systems, session management, and cloud deployments on Vercel.

Chapter III

Malayalam Open World 3D & AI Pipelines

Today in Plus One (+1), I am actively developing Malayalam Open World 3D — an interactive 3D game built right in the browser using Three.js and WebGL. It captures the authentic aesthetic of rural Kerala, complete with traditional tea shops ("Chayakada"), character sword rigging, and responsive physics.

Alongside this, I authored the Antigravity Blender MCP server, connecting AI agents directly with Blender 3D to automate procedural modeling, scene illumination, and snapshot rendering.
